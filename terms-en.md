# Airpost Terms of Service

Effective date: 2026-08-25


## 1. The Service

Airpost ("the Service") is a pen pal service for exchanging letters with people around the world. A letter takes time to arrive — as long as the distance between two hub airports.

## 2. Accounts

1. You must be at least 14 years old to use the Service.
2. Accounts are created with Apple or Google sign-in, and the information you provide at signup must be truthful.
3. You can delete your account at any time in Settings › Account › Delete Account.

## 3. Prohibited conduct — zero tolerance

The following conduct and content are prohibited. We apply a zero-tolerance policy: violations may lead to letters being hidden, or accounts being suspended or removed, without prior notice.

1. Harassment, threats, or hate speech
2. Unwanted sexual content
3. Spam, scams, or commercial advertising
4. Impersonation or unauthorized collection of personal information
5. Inappropriate contact with minors
6. Any other violation of applicable law

## 4. Reporting and blocking

1. You can report or block another user from the conversation menu — with a letter as evidence, or without one.
2. Reports are reviewed by the operator, who may hide letters, suspend accounts, or take other measures.
3. The original text of a reported letter is preserved for review.

## 5. Content

1. You own the content you write, including your letters.
2. The Service uses content only to the extent needed to operate — delivery, storage, and review.

## 6. Changes to the Service

If the Service changes or is discontinued, we will announce it in the app beforehand.

## 7. Limitation of liability

The Service does not pre-screen every exchanged letter. Disputes between users are handled through reasonable measures such as report review.
