3. Collection Methods and Legal Grounds

1. Information is obtained from User input and transmission, external sign-in providers, records generated through use of the Service, and support communications. 2. In Korea, processing necessary for account management and the requested Service relies on contract formation or performance under Article 15(1)(4) of the Personal Information Protection Act. Optional processing requiring consent relies on consent under Article 15(1)(1). 3. Processing for safety, security and protection of rights relies on Article 15(1)(6) only where it is necessary to achieve the Operator’s legitimate interest, that interest clearly overrides the User’s rights, and the processing is substantially related to that interest and does not exceed a reasonable scope. Processing to comply with legal obligations, such as retaining access records under the Protection of Communications Secrets Act, relies on Article 15(1)(2). 4. Operating-system permissions for location, microphone and notifications are distinct from legally required consent. A device permission does not replace separate consent where required. 5. Refusing necessary processing may prevent the relevant Service from being provided. Refusal of an optional feature’s information does not restrict unrelated features. 6. For Users outside Korea, the Operator relies on the grounds recognized by the applicable law: entering into and performing the contract to provide the Service the User requested, the User’s consent, legitimate interests in safety, security and protection of rights, and compliance with legal obligations.

The server’s automatic cleanup runs every minute and, separately, once a day directly in the database. Information in the table below that is subject to the server’s automatic cleanup is therefore deleted once its period ends, normally within minutes and at the latest within one day. However, deleting the stored files of expired uploads and report evidence, and account deletion, are handled by the every-minute cleanup. Information subject to a legal preservation duty is segregated from ordinary service data.

2. When account deletion is requested, new use is restricted at once, registered notification and Live Activity tokens are deleted, Find Pen Pals visibility and ID search are turned off, and deletion processing begins immediately. Deletion usually completes within minutes of the request but may take longer depending on the amount of attachments or server conditions, and if provider processing or a failure leaves work outstanding, the server retries it automatically. Acceptance and completion are separate stages, and the app shows the processing status. Processing works as follows. (1) For accounts that signed in with Apple, the Operator asks Apple to revoke the sign-in authorization (token revocation). The Operator does not ask Google to disconnect the account, so Users can remove the Airpost connection shown in their Google Account settings themselves. (2) Evidence copies of reported content are made under the report rules in the table in paragraph 1. (3) In the same step, the titles, text, audio and drawings of letters the Member sent are deleted and those letters are made unreadable, and delivery route information (airports and coordinates) is deleted for every letter the Member sent or received. The Member’s public letters lose their text and are withdrawn. Report details the Member wrote, public letter text copied into reports against the Member and the details of duplicate reports involving the Member are cleared. However, details already copied into an evidence copy because the reported Member deleted their account first are deleted under the report-evidence rule in the table in paragraph 1. The Member’s upload records, including those of files attached to letters the Member sent, are queued for file deletion and are deleted 30 days after the files are deleted. Date-of-birth, gender and language records are also deleted. The profile’s nickname is changed to “Deleted User” (“탈퇴한 사람” in Korean), and its biography, interests, country, city, hub airport and avatar are cleared. Pending pen-pal requests are deleted and other pen-pal relationships are ended. (4) After the audio and drawing files the Member uploaded are deleted from storage, the authentication account and the Apple or Google sign-in information linked to it are finally deleted. Sign-in sessions and refresh tokens, Terms acceptance and Policy acknowledgement records and the stored Apple token are deleted with it. 3. After deletion is completed, the following records remain only while another Member’s records need them, so that other Members’ mailboxes, pen-pal lists and block lists stay intact. The departed Member is shown to other Members as “Deleted User”, and letters the departed Member sent are shown as deleted because their author left. (1) Profile record: internal account ID, pen-pal ID (which cannot be searched and is shown to no one), the “Deleted User” label, status, sign-up, onboarding, last-update, deletion-request and deletion times, and the Find Pen Pals visibility and ID-search settings, both turned off (2) Records of letters the Member sent, without titles, text, audio or drawings: letter, sender, recipient, pen-pal relationship and request IDs, flight number, stamp, seal, font and aircraft IDs, public-letter link, moderation state, creation, sending, arrival and opening times, and the times landing-soon and arrival notifications were sent (3) Letters other Members sent to the Member: these include the text those Members wrote and remain as their sent letters, without delivery route information (4) Ended pen-pal relationships: both Members’ IDs, relationship kind and status, requesting Member, and request, creation, activation and end times (5) Block records in both directions: blocking Member, blocked Member and time, used to decide what the remaining Member sees, such as their block list and hidden letters and stamps (6) Public letter records whose text has been cleared, under the public letter rule in the table in paragraph 1 Stamps and seals that other Members received in the departed Member’s letters may continue to appear in their collections through the letter records in item (2). Report and sanction records are kept and deleted separately under the table in paragraph 1. Discovery and recommendation records involving the departed Member, that Member’s seal ownership records, retired friend-invite records and hidden-public-letter choices are deleted at the first cleanup after the content-deletion step of account deletion. 4. The records in paragraph 3 are deleted by automatic cleanup once every related Member has also completed their own deletion. Letters, pen-pal relationships and block records are deleted when every participant’s deletion has finished, but are kept while a report, duplicate-report or moderation record refers to the letter. The profile record is deleted once no letter, pen-pal relationship, block, public letter, report, duplicate-report or moderation record, or App Review demo-room setting refers to it and none of its notification jobs is unfinished. Its pen-pal ID is then kept only as a seven-character code in a list of retired IDs, with no link to any account, and is used only to ensure the same ID is never issued again. Nothing in this paragraph is cleaned up while deletion is still in progress (that is, while the authentication account has not yet been deleted). Letters that still have audio or drawing records, which can only remain for accounts removed outside the in-app deletion flow, such as from an administration console, are excluded from automatic cleanup and are checked and handled individually by the Operator. 5. Private letter drafts stored on the device, letters left in the device outbox after a failed send (including their titles, text, audio and drawings), reports not yet sent to the server and downloaded attachment caches are kept only on that device. A letter that failed to send stays on the device until the Member resends or deletes it, and all of this on-device information is deleted from the device on sign-out or withdrawal. However, a public letter draft in progress (its text and the selected stamp) is kept in the app’s settings storage, which is not separated by account and may be included in device backups, and it is not deleted on sign-out or withdrawal. The draft is deleted when the Member leaves the compose screen without saving or posts the public letter; until then it may also appear on the public letter compose screen of another account signed in on the same device. Revoking a device permission or uninstalling the app does not itself delete server accounts or content. 6. Electronic information is deleted using methods intended to prevent recovery or reproduction. Any physical copies are securely destroyed, for example by shredding. Restoring backups requires reapplying previously completed deletion requests. 7. Deleting information within the Service does not recall copies saved or forwarded outside it. Suspected rights infringements may be reported to the contact in Section 15. 8. Minimum records needed for ongoing reports, disputes or active sanctions are separately managed for a specific purpose and lawful basis, with periodic review of necessity. Once that reason ends, the rules above apply. This is not a general basis for indefinite retention. Separate statutory preservation duties follow the relevant law and period.

8. Location and Device Permissions

1. If location access is permitted, the current location is used only on the device to suggest nearby airports. To look up the city name of the current location or of a point chosen on the map, those coordinates are sent to Apple’s map service. Current-location coordinates are not sent to or stored on the Operator’s servers; the Operator stores only the hub airport the User selects and the city name identified this way. 2. The microphone is used when the User records audio. Recordings are made on the device, and only a recording attached to a letter is uploaded while that letter is being sent. Recordings in letters that fail to send stay in the device outbox until the letter is resent or deleted (Section 4(5)), and unsent uploads left on the server follow the rule in Section 4(1). 3. Notification permission enables service alerts. Permissions can be changed in device settings; this does not automatically delete content stored on servers. 4. To keep the country shown on profiles reliable, whenever a hub airport is chosen or changed the Service checks the connection country that the hosting provider derives from the IP address, whether or not location access is permitted. If airports are available in that country, only those airports can be chosen. If the country cannot be determined or has no available airport, this restriction does not apply. The connection country is used only for this check and is not stored with the account.
