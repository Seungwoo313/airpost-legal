# Airpost Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-25


Airpost ("the Service") handles personal information as follows.

## 1. What we collect

- Account: your Apple or Google sign-in identifier and sign-in email
- Profile: nickname, date of birth, gender, interests, languages and levels, bio, avatar configuration, hub airport, city name (optional)
- Content: letter titles and bodies, report details
- Device: push notification token
- Location: used once during signup to suggest nearby airports. Coordinates are never stored or sent to the server; only the airport you choose and a city name are saved.

## 2. Why we use it

- Connecting and recommending pen pals (based on interests, languages, country)
- Delivering letters and arrival notifications
- Your public profile — nickname, age (your birth date itself is never shown), gender, country, interests and languages are visible to other users. Your city name is visible only to you.
- Safety: handling reports, blocking, restricting accounts

## 3. How long we keep it

- When you delete your account, your profile, birth date, gender and other personal information are deleted or anonymized without delay.
- The letters you have already exchanged remain in the recipient's mailbox — a letter belongs to its recipient too.
- The original text of a reported letter is preserved for review.

## 4. Third parties and processors

- We never sell or share personal information with third parties. The app contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.
- Processors: Supabase, Inc. (data hosting — servers in the AWS ap-northeast-2 region, Republic of Korea), Apple (push delivery and sign-in), Google (sign-in)

## 5. Minimum age

You must be at least 14 years old to use the Service.

## 6. Your rights

You can edit your profile in the app and delete your account in Settings › Account. For access or correction requests, contact us below.

## 7. Security

Traffic is encrypted (HTTPS) and server access is controlled with row-level security policies.
